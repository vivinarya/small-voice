# National Public School, ITPL (NPS ITPL)

National Public School ITPL is a premier CBSE-affiliated school located in the Whitefield area of Bangalore, Karnataka, India. It is one of the flagship campuses of the NPS Group of Institutions.

## Location
The school is situated on a 4.2-acre campus at Goravigere, Kadugodi Main Road, Bengaluru – 560067. It is located near ITPL (International Tech Park Limited) in the Whitefield and Kadugodi area, making it accessible to families in the Bangalore tech corridor.

## Affiliation and Establishment
- Established: 2018
- Affiliated with: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi
- CBSE Affiliation Number: 831091
- Part of the NPS Group of Institutions, founded by Dr. K. P. Gopalkrishna

## Curriculum and Approach
The school follows a Montessori methodology for early years (Kindergarten) which transitions to the CBSE curriculum for Grades 1 through 12. This blended approach focuses on nurturing curiosity, critical thinking, and holistic development.

## School Timings
- Montessori and Kindergarten: 8:00 AM to 12:30 PM
- Grades 1 to 12: 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM
